HSE Hockey

Overview

HSE Hockey is a high school hockey program based in Fishers, Indiana. The program gives students the opportunity to compete in organized hockey while representing Hamilton Southeastern and Fishers High School. HSE Hockey is about more than what happens on the ice. Players learn the importance of teamwork, communication, discipline, leadership, responsibility, and sportsmanship. The goal is to help athletes become better hockey players while also developing qualities that will help them in school, their community, and beyond. The program has developed a strong tradition of competitive hockey and continues to work toward building successful teams and developing players for the future.

Championships & Achievements

HSE Hockey has built a strong tradition of success through hard work and dedication. The program has competed for championships at the state and league levels. One of the program's recent accomplishments came in 2026, when HSE Hockey won the Indiana State Championship and the Hoosier League Championship. These accomplishments represent the work of the players, coaches, families, and supporters who have helped make HSE Hockey successful. The goal is not only to celebrate past championships but also to continue building a program capable of competing for championships in the future.

Teams

HSE A Team

The HSE A Team is the program's top competitive team, featuring skilled players who compete against strong high school hockey programs. The team focuses on teamwork, discipline, player development, and competing for championships.

HSE B Team

The HSE B Team provides players with a competitive environment where they can develop their hockey skills and gain valuable game experience. The team emphasizes teamwork, improvement, and preparation for higher levels of competition.

HSE JV Team

The HSE JV Team focuses on developing younger and developing players through practices and games. Players work on their fundamentals, gain experience, and learn the teamwork and discipline needed to succeed in high school hockey.
